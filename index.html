A bit more about me

I like the unglamorous middle of AI systems: the data an agent stands on. At work that means modeling entities, resolving conflicting sources (code and docs win; chats and emails update), and building the loops that let a system notice when it's wrong.

Outside of work I tinker on my own assistant and dig into research on retrieval and knowledge — the problems I think will matter most as agents take on more real work.
